# Changelog

## [1.0] — 2026-07-03 · versionCode 1

### Added
- Auto-detects your current location on launch and shows its weather, local time, and map
- Search any city or place worldwide (Open-Meteo geocoding)
- Live current weather: temperature, feels-like, humidity, wind, day/night-aware condition
- World Time tab: live clocks for your chosen cities, with day/night icons and UTC offsets
- Weather-concept theme: dynamic sky gradient matched to the current conditions
- Location shown on an OpenStreetMap (osmdroid) map
- Feedback (WhatsApp) and About tabs in the Tertiary Infotech house style

### Play submission
- First release: Closed testing track, All Testers list.
